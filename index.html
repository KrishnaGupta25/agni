05Placement & Internship Readiness

Prepare for the Coding Rounds That Matter.

Build the fundamentals and problem-solving confidence needed to prepare for internship and placement coding rounds. AGNI focuses on the skills those rounds test — clean C++, recognising patterns, reasoning about complexity and solving under time pressure.

AGNI helps you prepare. We do not guarantee internships, placements, salary packages or interview selection — outcomes depend on individual effort, skills and hiring processes.

  1. Stronger programming fundamentals

    Write correct, readable C++ with confidence.

  2. Common DSA patterns

    Two pointers, sliding window, hashing, recursion, binary search and more.

  3. Time & space complexity

    Analyse and justify the efficiency of your solutions.

  4. Structured problem practice

    Topic-wise coding problems that build depth.

  5. Timed problem-solving sessions

    Practise thinking clearly against the clock.

  6. Mock interview practice

    Explain your approach out loud, the way interviews expect.

  7. Topic-wise revision

    Consolidate everything before coding assessments.
